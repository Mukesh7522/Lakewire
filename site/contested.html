Where editors are clashing

A page is flagged when different editors alternate within ten minutes at least three times in a day. It is a signal worth a look, not a verdict on any editor or article.

Demo data. Nothing here is real. These numbers are simulated so you can look around before the first snapshot is published.
What is countedBack-and-forth edits by different people within ten minutes of each other.
How it scoresRapid reversals, multiplied by the number of distinct editors (capped at five).
What it is notIt does not say who is right. Plenty of heavily edited pages are simply breaking news.
