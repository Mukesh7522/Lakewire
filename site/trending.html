# Article Views vs yesterday Edits Score

How the score works

Trend score is the natural log of an article's views for the hour, multiplied by its growth against the same hour the day before. The log keeps one giant page from drowning out everything else, and the growth term lifts pages that are rising rather than just popular.

Source: Wikimedia EventStreams and pageview dumps. Built with Lakewire (full stack mode).
